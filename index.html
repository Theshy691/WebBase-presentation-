Section I • Governance

Business Rules & Ethical Framework

FIDUCIARY CHARACTER

Priority Calibrated to Reglementary Deadlines

Rather than an arbitrary first-come-first-served queue, case urgency is calibrated to court deadlines, prescriptive periods, and reglementary appeal periods.

Rule: "Backlog is not an option as legal ethics mandates that a lawyer's duty should be performed within the timeframe provided by law."

Perpetual Duty of Confidentiality

The lawyer-client relationship is of the highest fiduciary character. The obligation of absolute confidentiality persists indefinitely, even after termination of legal representation.

Data Privacy: Compliance with Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Sensitive personal records are stored physically on-premise.

Daily End-of-Day Review

All staff communications, calendar intakes, and client filings are reported to Atty. Belde before close of business each day.

Conflict-of-Interest Clearance

Mandatory cross-examination of prospective vs existing client rosters prior to creating any digital record or retaining legal fees.

Section II • Stakeholders

Audience & Role-Based Access

TWO-TIER ARCHITECTURE

Primary Audience: Internal Personnel

ADMIN / OPERATIONAL

Atty. Allan P. Belde (Owner & Solo Practitioner): Directs all legal strategy, reviews legal research, approves drafted pleadings, conducts court appearances, and performs daily compliance reviews.

Legal Secretaries: Intake prospective clients, execute conflict-of-interest checks, encode records, manage the centralized appointment calendar, and dispatch case updates.

Secondary Audience: The Clients

SELF-SERVICE

Prospective & Active Clients: Individuals seeking legal assistance in Pangasinan and surrounding regions. Need clear, real-time visibility into their case stages without requiring physical office visits for simple updates.

Restricted Privileges: Clients can view only their own records, request appointments, and access financial receipts via verified credentials.

Section II • Compliance

Data Privacy Shield (RA 10173)

ETHICAL SAFEGUARD
PRINCIPLE 1

Minimalist Cloud Footprint

The online portal stores strictly necessary contact parameters: Name, Email, Contact Number, and Contact Person.

PRINCIPLE 2

On-Premise Vault

Sensitive evidence, birth certificates, and financial affidavits are collected directly at the physical law office, eliminating external breach exposure.

PRINCIPLE 3

OTP Phone Callback

Inquiries regarding remaining balances trigger an automated OTP. Once validated, an authorized legal secretary executes an out-of-band telephone call to disclose figures.

Security Controls Implemented: Password hashing, role-based access control (RBAC), automatic inactivity session termination, and daily automated encrypted database backups.

Primary Field Research: Interview Transcript

Verbatim responses from Atty. Allan P. Belde, Founder & Solo Practitioner (July 2004 – Present).

Q: What is the law office's current mission and vision?

"The mission and vision is to uphold the rule of law and represent the client with fidelity and zeal."

Q: What rules do you follow in prioritizing cases?

"The office observes diligence and punctuality in all appearances, submissions, meetings, and other commitments. There is no rigid hierarchy of prioritization as it is calibrated to court deadlines, prescriptive, reglementary, and appeal periods."

Q: Why is confidentiality of clients important?

"The lawyer-client relationship is of the highest fiduciary character; as a trust relation, it is essential that the engagement is founded on the confidence reposed by the client on the lawyer. Without confidence the client might withhold information or facts necessary in handling a case."

Q: Does the office experience backlogs? How do you handle them?

"Backlog is not an option as legal ethics mandates that a lawyer’s duty should be performed within the timeframe provided by law, otherwise your case or your client's rights will be compromised."

Q: What is the current process for managing client records?

"It is still manually being placed on steel cabinets but we are trying to adopt or shift to a computer-based system of managing and organizing clients' records."
