FOLIO 08 // INFORMATION SECURITY

Data Privacy Shield & RA 10173 Compliance

NATIONAL PRIVACY COMMISSION
SAFEGUARD 01

Minimalist Cloud Storage

The portal database stores strictly minimal contact credentials: Name, Email Address, Contact Number, and Case ID. No sensitive personal affidavits are hosted online.

SAFEGUARD 02

Physical On-Premise Vault

Sensitive evidence, birth certificates, and financial affidavits are collected directly at the physical law office in Umingan, eliminating cloud data breach exposures.

SAFEGUARD 03

OTP Phone Callback

Financial balance inquiries trigger an automated 6-digit OTP. Once validated, an authorized legal secretary executes an out-of-band phone call before disclosing amounts.

Controls Implemented: SHA-256 password hashing, TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, 15-minute idle session termination, daily encrypted backups, and comprehensive audit logs.

Primary Field Research: Interview Transcript

Verbatim responses from Atty. Allan P. Belde, Founder & Solo Practitioner (July 2004 – Present).

12 CORE RESEARCH INQUIRIES
Q01: Mission & Vision Charter CORE VALUES

"What is the law office's foundational mission and vision in serving Umingan?"

"The mission and vision is to uphold the rule of law and represent the client with fidelity and zeal."

Q02: Case Prioritization & Reglementary Deadlines ETHICAL DILIGENCE

"What formal rules do you follow in prioritizing pending matters?"

"The office observes diligence and punctuality in all appearances, submissions, meetings, and other commitments. There is no rigid hierarchy of prioritization as it is calibrated to court deadlines, prescriptive, reglementary, and appeal periods."

Q03: Client Confidentiality & Fiduciary Character CODE OF ETHICS

"Why is strict confidentiality of clients paramount in legal practice?"

"The lawyer-client relationship is of the highest fiduciary character; as a trust relation, it is essential that the engagement is founded on the confidence reposed by the client on the lawyer. Without confidence the client might withhold information or facts necessary in handling a case. This fiduciary duty continues perpetually even after the case terminates."

Q04: Operational Backlog Tolerance STATUTORY COMPLIANCE

"Does the chambers experience backlogs, and how are they managed?"

"Backlog is not an option as legal ethics mandates that a lawyer’s duty should be performed within the timeframe provided by law, otherwise your case or your client's rights will be compromised."

Q05: Current Records Management System AS-IS ANALYSIS

"What is the current method for archiving and retrieving client folders?"

"It is still manually being placed on steel cabinets but we are trying to adopt or shift to a computer-based system of managing and organizing clients' records to prevent wear, loss, and search delays."

Q06: Client Communication Friction COMMUNICATION GAPS

"How do clients currently inquire about the status of their cases?"

"Clients reach out via personal phone calls or Messenger chats at unpredictable hours, sometimes while counsel is actively in trial before the judge. A synchronized online tracking portal will allow clients to review case stages 24/7 without interrupting ongoing courtroom trials."

Q07: Court Trial Jurisdictions Covered PRACTICE SCOPE

"What court jurisdictions does your practice regularly attend?"

"We handle litigation primarily across the Regional Trial Court Branch 42 in Tayug, as well as branches in Rosales and Urdaneta City, alongside Municipal Trial Courts in Umingan, San Quintin, and neighboring towns."

Q08: Conflict-of-Interest Screening LEGAL ETHICS

"How do you evaluate potential representation conflicts before accepting retainers?"

"Before accepting any case or consultation fee, we rigorously verify the adverse party. Representing opposing parties—even indirectly—violates legal ethics and damages public trust in the legal profession. An automated pre-screening check protects both the firm and the prospective client."

Q09: Pro Bono & Community Counsel COMMUNITY SERVICE

"Does the firm maintain commitments for indigent community members?"

"Yes. Part of our practice provides free legal assistance and counseling to qualified indigent clients in Umingan. Legal defense must remain accessible regardless of financial stature."

Q10: Data Privacy & Physical Document Safes RA 10173 SAFEGUARDS

"How should a digital system balance accessibility with statutory data privacy?"

"A law office handles highly sensitive family disputes and original Torrens land titles. Original evidentiary instruments must stay in fireproof on-premise safes, while the web system manages schedules, basic coordinates, and encrypted milestones under the Data Privacy Act."

Q11: Certified Retainer Receipts & Transparency ACCOUNTING DILIGENCE

"Why is rapid electronic receipting important for clients?"

"Clear, itemized billing breakdowns reassure clients on exactly what court pleading, appearance, or notarial act their funds are applied to, complying with both Supreme Court standards and BIR official receipting requirements."

Q12: End-of-Day Roster Dispatch Protocol EXECUTIVE OVERSIGHT

"How does the solo practitioner maintain control over daily administrative volume?"

"At the close of business, all new client inquiries, registered notarial instruments, and court deadlines for the coming week must be consolidated and verified. No court appearance or pleading deadline can slip through."
