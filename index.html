Business Rules & Fiduciary Obligations

ETHICAL MANDATE

Priority Calibrated to Court Deadlines

Urgency is determined by statutory court deadlines, prescriptive periods, and reglementary appeal timelines rather than an arbitrary first-come queue.

"Backlog is not an option as legal ethics mandates that a lawyer's duty must be performed within the timeframe provided by law."

Perpetual Duty of Confidentiality

The lawyer-client relationship is of the highest fiduciary character. The duty of absolute confidentiality continues perpetually, surviving the termination of representation.

Data Privacy: Compliance with Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173). Sensitive personal records remain physically on-premise.

Daily End-of-Day Review

All staff communications, calendar intakes, and client filings are reported to Atty. Belde before close of business each day.

Conflict-of-Interest Clearance

Mandatory cross-examination of prospective vs existing client rosters prior to creating any digital record or retaining legal fees.

Stakeholder Matrix & Access Controls

RBAC MODEL

Primary Audience: Internal Personnel

ADMINISTRATIVE

Atty. Allan P. Belde (Owner & Solo Practitioner): Directs legal strategy, reviews research, approves pleadings, conducts court appearances, and performs daily compliance reviews.

Legal Secretaries: Intake prospective clients, execute conflict-of-interest checks, encode records, manage centralized calendaring, and dispatch case updates.

Secondary Audience: The Clients

SELF-SERVICE

Prospective & Active Clients: Community members seeking legal counsel. Need real-time visibility into case progress without needing in-person office visits for simple milestones.

Access Boundary: Clients are strictly restricted to their own individual records, appointment scheduling, and verified financial receipts.

Data Privacy Shield & RA 10173 Compliance

SECURITY CONTROLS
PRINCIPLE 01

Minimalist Cloud Storage

The portal database stores strictly minimal contact credentials: Name, Email Address, Contact Number, and Designated Contact Person.

PRINCIPLE 02

Physical On-Premise Vault

Sensitive evidence, birth certificates, and financial affidavits are collected directly at the physical law office, eliminating external cyber breach risks.

PRINCIPLE 03

OTP Phone Callback

Financial balance inquiries trigger an automated OTP. Once validated, an authorized legal secretary executes an out-of-band telephone call to disclose figures.

Security Controls Implemented: User authentication, password hashing, role-based access control (RBAC), automatic inactivity session termination, and daily automated encrypted database backups.

Primary Field Research: Interview Transcript

Verbatim responses from Atty. Allan P. Belde, Founder & Solo Practitioner (July 2004 – Present).

Q: What is the law office's current mission and vision?

"The mission and vision is to uphold the rule of law and represent the client with fidelity and zeal."

Q: What rules do you follow in prioritizing cases?

"The office observes diligence and punctuality in all appearances, submissions, meetings, and other commitments. There is no rigid hierarchy of prioritization as it is calibrated to court deadlines, prescriptive, reglementary, and appeal periods."

Q: Why is confidentiality of clients important?

"The lawyer-client relationship is of the highest fiduciary character; as a trust relation, it is essential that the engagement is founded on the confidence reposed by the client on the lawyer. Without confidence the client might withhold information or facts necessary in handling a case."

Q: Does the office experience backlogs? How do you handle them?

"Backlog is not an option as legal ethics mandates that a lawyer’s duty should be performed within the timeframe provided by law, otherwise your case or your client's rights will be compromised."

Q: What is the current process for managing client records?

"It is still manually being placed on steel cabinets but we are trying to adopt or shift to a computer-based system of managing and organizing clients' records."
